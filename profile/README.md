# BuzzCloud

**Local-first, proximity-based community networking.**

BuzzCloud is an experimental platform for building decentralized, modular community networks that connect people, devices, and local infrastructure.

Our goal is to develop technology that supports meaningful human interactions while preserving individual and community autonomy.

## Our Principles

- **Local-first:** Essential functionality should remain available without an Internet connection.
- **Proximity-based trust:** Physical proximity helps establish relationships and interactions.
- **Modular infrastructure:** Components can be combined and expanded to meet community needs.
- **Community autonomy:** Individuals and communities retain control over their participation and infrastructure.
- **Open architecture:** Clearly defined responsibilities and interfaces support interoperability and future development.

## Architecture

BuzzCloud is organized around five complementary components:

- **Cell** — An individual's node and customizable interaction environment.
- **Stinger** — A proximity-based credential for identity and authentication across devices.
- **Honeycomb** — A lightweight edge-network node connecting Cells and other Honeycombs.
- **Hive** — Larger network infrastructure offering expanded services and connectivity.
- **Queen** — Cloud-based services supporting updates, software distribution, and network coordination.

## Development

BuzzCloud is currently in early development, with initial engineering focused on the Honeycomb edge-node prototype.

Project repositories, technical documentation, and contribution information will be published as development progresses.
